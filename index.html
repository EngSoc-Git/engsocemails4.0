Additional Wellness Support for Engineering Students at the University of Guelph

As the Student Wellness Navigator supporting the College of Engineering, Erika provides individualized, short-term support to help students navigate university life and prioritize their overall well-being.

Whether you're experiencing academic stress, burnout, challenges with social connection, or looking to improve your overall wellness, we can discuss your needs, explore and identify available resources, and determine what supports may be helpful.

Option 1: Book an Appointment

Engineering students have access to dedicated Thursday afternoon appointments with a Student Wellness Navigator.

  • Duration: 30 minutes
  • Location: In person at the J.T. Powell Building or virtually
  • Book your appointment here.
Thursdays don't work for you? Email ebeaver@uoguelph.ca directly at to arrange an appointment on an alternative date.

Option 2: Engineering Wellness Drop-In

Stop by for an informal conversation! Drop-ins are an opportunity to:
  • Say hello and learn more about Wellness Navigation.
  • Check in about how things are going.
  • Explore campus wellness supports and resources.
  • Get assistance booking an appointment.
  • No appointment necessary - just drop by!
  • Times: Tuesdays | 1:30-3:30 p.m.
  • Dates: November 17 - December 15 (no drop-ins Dec. 1)
  • Location: In person at the J.T. Powell Building or virtually
  • Book your appointment here.
