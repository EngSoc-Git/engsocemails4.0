6th Annual STEM Sustainability Case Competition
SYSTEMS OF TOMORROW

Interested in scientific research, sustainability, or tackling real-world challenges? This is your opportunity to gain valuable research experience, develop your skills, and compete for a first-place prize of $1,000!

What is the competition?

The competition challenges undergraduate students to develop innovative research proposals addressing global challenges through an interdisciplinary STEM approach.

Students can register individually or as a team of up to three. Those who register individually will be assigned to a team, so no need to worry if you don't have teammates! Each team will also be paired with a graduate student mentor and have access to workshops to support their research and presentation skills.

Competition Timeline

Round 1: Research Abstract

• November 19 - December 10, 2026: Abstract submission deadline @ 11:59PM
• January 2027: Announcement of the top 20 teams advancing to Round 2

Round 2: Scientific Poster Presentation

• March 23, 2027 (tentative): In-person conference day at the University of Guelph. Finalists will present their research proposals to a panel of faculty judges.

Prizes and Opportunities

🥇 1st place: $1,000
🥈 2nd place: $500
🥉 3rd place: $250

Participants will also gain hands-on research experience, receive mentorship, develop valuable communication and presentation skills, and connect with members of the STEM community. The top 20 teams will also have their abstracts published in URNCST, an international undergraduate research journal.

Interested in Participating?

Students can register individually or with a team. Please note that a $25 registration deposit per person is required to participate.

CALLING ALL MECHANICAL ENGINEERS!

The Canadian Society for Mechanical Engineering (CSME) University of Guelph Chapter is pleased to announce its inaugural CSME Student Medal program, recognizing outstanding achievement among Mechanical Engineering students at the University of Guelph.

Three medals will be awarded this year:

  • Junior Medal — open to students in 1st or 2nd year
  • Senior Medal — open to students in 3rd, 4th, or 5th+ year
  • Graduate Medal — open to Master's and PhD students
Selection criteria include:
  • Academic excellence (minimum GPA of 3.0)
  • Engagement through design teams, projects, labs, or extracurricular engineering activities
  • Demonstrated leadership, mentorship, or contribution to the program and community
🗓️ Application deadline: November 21, 2026

Winners will be announced in late November/early December and recognized at an award event, with refreshments provided.
