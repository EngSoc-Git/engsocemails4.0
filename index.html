Entrepreneurship Programs

Applications are now open for IgniteLab and VentureLab and will close on September 20. Whether you're developing an innovative, scalable venture or growing a business, side hustle, or freelance service, we have a program designed to support your goals.
  • Hub Incubator - IgniteLab: A 10-week incubator program designed for students developing innovative and scalable business ideas. Participants work through customer discovery, market validation, and venture development with support from mentors and entrepreneurs-in-residence.
  • VentureLab: A practical entrepreneurship program for students building businesses, side hustles, freelance ventures, and service-based companies. Gain the skills, coaching, and accountability needed to launch and grow your venture.
  • 1:1 Business Coaching: Building a service-based business, side gig, innovative product, or just want general business advice? Book a 20-minute meeting to chat with our Entrepreneur-in-Residence!
We're also hosting information sessions for our hub incubator and VentureLab programs on September 14 and September 17. These sessions are a great opportunity to learn more about each program, ask questions, and determine which pathway is the right fit for you.

Guelph Engineering Competition 2026

What if you could put your engineering skills to the test beyond the classroom?

Registration is now open for the Guelph Engineering Competition (GEC) 2026!

Taking place October 3-4, GEC is a two-day, student-led engineering competition bringing together 200+ undergraduate engineering students to tackle real-world challenges through creativity, collaboration, and innovation.

🌎 Full Circle: Rethinking Resources This year's theme challenges you to rethink how resources are used, reused, and optimized. Work with a team to develop solutions that prioritize sustainability, efficiency, and long-term impact.

With 6 competition categories, there's a challenge for everyone:
🧬 Bio-Engineering: 2 people per team
🏗️ Senior Design: 4 people per team
⚙️ Junior Design: 4 people per team
🔧 Re-Engineering: 2 people per team
📊 Consulting: 4 people per team
💻 Programming: 4 people per team

Whether you're designing a prototype, improving an existing product, developing a software solution, or presenting to a mock client panel, GEC gives you the opportunity to take what you've learned in class and apply it to a real engineering challenge.

👉Register here!

Follow @guelphengineeringcompetition on Instagram for category spotlights, event updates, and more.
