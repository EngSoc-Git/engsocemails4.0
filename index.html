National Society of Black Engineers - UofG Chapter

NSBE is coming to Guelph!

Yes, you read that right! The University of Guelph is getting its very own National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE) chapter!

As Guelph's Black student community continues to grow, we're excited to build a safe and welcoming space to amplify the voices of students of colour in the College of Engineering, foster meaningful connections, and strengthen our sense of community on campus.

Whether you're looking to get involved, connect with others in the field, or support the community as an ally, everyone is welcome!

Stay tuned for more information on how to get involved through our Instagram, @nsbe_uoguelph, and keep an eye out for our upcoming event in your weekly newsletter.

We can't wait to build this community with you! 🖤💛❤️

LEED v5 Green Accreditation

Kick off the school year by becoming one of the first LEED v5 Green Associate professionals! While LEED is best known as the premier scorecard used to rate Green Buildings, individuals can also become LEED accredited to demonstrate their certified expertise in sustainability to employers.

Our workshops go beyond simply passing the test; they give you a strong, practical understanding of sustainable building to apply in the real world.

Be LEED v5 Ready: Our training has been extensively updated for the newly released LEED v5 rating system, requirements, and exam format.

Open to All: Students from any major can participate with no prior experience required.

Comprehensive Support: Get a study guide, 400 updated v5 practice questions, class recordings, and on-demand assistance from our LEED AP+ staff.

Trusted Provider: LeadingGreen has helped prepare over 18,000 candidates pass their exams on the first try!

Career-Boosting: This industry-recognized credential opens doors in architecture, engineering, construction, consulting, real estate, and more.

Rocketry First General Meeting

Welcome back everyone! UofG Rocketry is hosting our first general meeting this week!

Come join us there to learn about each of our projects:

  • Launch Canada High Powered Rocket: A custom carbon fiber airframe aiming to reach Mach 2.2 and an altitude of 10,000 m, paired with an in-house designed and assembled flight computer
  • CubeSat and HAB Project: Building multiple high altitude balloons that touch the edge of space followed by a student built satellite that will be launched to Earth orbit, our first project or HAB-1 is planned for launch in November where it will carry a camera and multiple environmental sensors up to the upper stratosphere!! (30 km+)
  • Liquid Propulsion Project: A mission to build a student-researched and designed liquid propellant engine using ethanol and nitrous oxide, driven by a gas turbopump
We look forward to seeing everyone there!
